# Task 5e: why detector 5 has no standard-practice baseline

Detector 5 targets aggregate cross-site scores whose gap is partly produced by
which components are computable at each site rather than by how well they hold.
The obvious standard check is the naive cross-site gap in that same aggregate
score. That quantity is exactly what detector 5 decomposes into a naive gap and a
composition-only gap, so comparing the two is not an independent test: the
baseline is an input to the detector, not a rival to it. For that reason no
baseline was run for detector 5, and the comparison section of the paper covers
detector 2's scenario only. Nothing here claims detector 5 outperforms ordinary
practice.
