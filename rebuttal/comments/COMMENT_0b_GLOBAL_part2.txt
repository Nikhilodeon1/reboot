Global comment, part 2 of 2: what we ran, planned revisions, fit.

What we ran. Every experiment below was pre-registered in a committed document before it ran. The anonymized repository, https://anonymous.4open.science/r/reboot-8568/, holds those documents with their commit hashes and times, the ledger of every number we quote, and the result files, in its rebuttal directory. The commit history itself is withheld because commit metadata identifies the authors, and it will be released with the de-anonymized repository.
- Detector 3: we enumerated candidate selection sites in the 18 repositories with patterns frozen before the scan (103 sites), sampled 53, and labelled them with a rubric committed before any site was read and before the detector was run: 0 contaminated, 1 disclosed oracle, 1 ambiguous, 21 sound and 30 not selections. Sensitivity is undefined because the sample has no contaminated site, and the detector returned indeterminate on 53 of 53 files.
- Type 1: 236 candidate sites, of which 88 were sampled, across 25 repositories, with the star-ranked selection rule fixed before any query.
- Detector 2: values-versus-missingness arms on the demonstration pair (our pre-stated prediction of a mixed result was wrong; the missingness share was 0.02 and -0.11 at 5% and 20%), the corpus-size control, and the 20-replicate null.
- Baselines: the three standard checks on the same trained models as detector 2, at both scales. At demonstration scale they flag at 0% leakage (k-fold 5/5, gap 4/4 (1 undecidable), floor 4/5). At full scale they behave differently (flags out of 5 seeds at 0, 5, 20 and 100% leakage: k-fold 0, 0, 0, 0; train/test gap 5, 4, 5, 4; external floor 0, 0, 0, 2). None carries information about this scenario, in different ways at the two scales.
- Detector 5: per-seed flagship ratios were archived and the Type 2 model was checked against them, exploratorily.

Planned revisions. We will make these changes in the revised version.
- Replace the abstract's headline performance sentence, and the sentence beginning "We argue the failure modes generalize", with: "The external evidence supports Type 1 only: a falsy-as-clean pattern was reachable and unguarded in 4 of 48 applicable sampled sites across 25 repositories (exact 95% interval 0.023 to 0.200), or 16 of 48 (0.204 to 0.484) if NaNs that arise only from diverged training count, and we found no evidence that it concentrates in code that renders verdicts."
- Replace "taxonomy" with "initial catalogue of failure modes, organized by the stage of a diagnostic at which they arise", and replace "Each type has a measured instance here" with "Types 1 to 3 have measured instances in our detectors, Type 1 also has an external structural instance, and Type 4 is supported counterfactually; we do not claim the catalogue is complete."
- Replace the detector 2 scope statement with: "Under the pre-registered rule, detector 2's effect is confounded with corpus size at full scale: a control that adds extra source stays in place of target stays recovers 0.55 and 0.69 of the effect at 5% and 20% leakage (0.31 and 0.48 at demonstration scale), above our pre-registered line of 0.5; target exposure still adds beyond volume, and the detector is unchanged."
- Add a stage model and a formal predicate for each type, a list of failure classes the catalogue does not cover, and an evidence-strength table; per-detector counts with exact intervals in the main results table, with the pooled row marked reference only; a validation-depth matrix; and the comparison with the tool of Yang et al.
