\section{Introduction}
\label{sec:intro}

A clinical machine learning result can be wrong in a way that nothing in the
paper reveals. The model is sound, the code runs, the numbers are real, and the
finding is an artifact of how the data was assembled. Systematic reviews of
COVID-19 prediction models found essentially none fit for clinical
use~\citep{wynants2020prediction,roberts2021common}, and the failures were
overwhelmingly of this kind\DIFdelbegin \DIFdel{: not modeling error, but confounded construction}\DIFdelend . Leakage in particular has been argued to underlie a
reproducibility crisis \DIFdelbegin \DIFdel{spanning many scientific }\DIFdelend \DIFaddbegin \DIFadd{across }\DIFaddend fields that use machine
learning~\citep{kapoor2023leakage}.

The natural response is tooling\DIFdelbegin \DIFdel{. If }\DIFdelend \DIFaddbegin \DIFadd{: if }\DIFaddend a confound leaves a detectable signature,

%DIFDELCMD <

\DIFdelbegin \paragraph{\DIFdel{Type 1 --- Vacuous pass.}} %DIFAUXCMD

\DIFdel{A check that never reached a verdict is

\paragraph{Detectors.} Detector~1 computes Cohen's $\kappa$~\citep{cohen1960kappa}
on a fixed 500-patient audit subset between two operationalizations of the
outcome \DIFdelbegin \DIFdel{, }\DIFdelend and flags below the conventional Landis--Koch
\DIFdelbegin \DIFdel{``substantial agreement''
}\DIFdelend boundary of $0.60$~\citep{landis1977measurement}\DIFdelbegin \DIFdel{. The }\DIFdelend \DIFaddbegin \DIFadd{; the }\DIFaddend reference definition is
window-mode SOFA per Sepsis-3~\citep{singer2016sepsis3}\DIFdelbegin \DIFdel{, which specifies a SOFA
rise over an interval around the suspicion time rather than a reading at one
instant}\DIFdelend . Detector~2 trains a
small masked-reconstruction probe on a source site with a controlled fraction of
target\DIFdelbegin \DIFdel{-site}\DIFdelend  stays leaked in \DIFdelbegin \DIFdel{, }\DIFdelend and tests whether probe loss on held-out target data
falls\DIFdelbegin \DIFdel{; the test is }\DIFdelend \DIFaddbegin \DIFadd{, with }\DIFaddend a one-sided paired $t$ within seed \DIFdelbegin \DIFdel{with $df$ one less than the seed count (}\DIFdelend \DIFaddbegin \DIFadd{($df=4$ for }\DIFaddend 5 seeds\DIFdelbegin \DIFdel{, $df=4$}\DIFdelend ). Detector~3
is static analysis: \DIFdelbegin \DIFdel{it traces, }\DIFdelend via Python's abstract syntax tree \DIFdelbegin \DIFdel{, }\DIFdelend \DIFaddbegin \DIFadd{it traces }\DIFaddend whether a sweep
function's evaluation call receives a loader derived from the held-out
distribution. Detector~4 traces variable lineage through the same \DIFdelbegin \DIFdel{AST }\DIFdelend machinery,
with guard-condition reasoning, to find constraints imposed on variables derived
from those \DIFdelbegin \DIFdel{same }\DIFdelend constraints. Detector~5 compares per-component availability ratios
and a composition-controlled aggregate across sites\DIFaddbegin \DIFadd{, and flags when the
availability ratio exceeds $2.0$ and the composition gap ratio exceeds $0.30$}\DIFaddend .

\paragraph{Ground truth.} \DIFdelbegin \DIFdel{Wherever possible the }\DIFdelend \DIFaddbegin \DIFadd{The }\DIFaddend positive case is a real historical defect \DIFdelbegin \DIFdel{. Detector}\DIFdelend \DIFaddbegin \DIFadd{where
possible: detector}\DIFaddend ~3's \DIFdelbegin \DIFdel{positive is a specific }\DIFdelend \DIFaddbegin \DIFadd{is a }\DIFaddend buggy sweep from our \DIFdelbegin \DIFdel{own
}\DIFdelend git history, \DIFdelbegin \DIFdel{retained verbatim alongside }\DIFdelend \DIFaddbegin \DIFadd{kept with }\DIFaddend its
corrected version\DIFdelbegin \DIFdel{. Detector}\DIFdelend \DIFaddbegin \DIFadd{; detector}\DIFaddend ~4's \DIFdelbegin \DIFdel{positive }\DIFdelend is a genuine Severinghaus circularity in our
PhysioNet loader\DIFdelbegin \DIFdel{, where
PaO\textsubscript{2} is derived from SaO\textsubscript{2} and then constrained
against it. Detector}\DIFdelend \DIFaddbegin \DIFadd{; detector}\DIFaddend ~1's \DIFdelbegin \DIFdel{positive }\DIFdelend is ICD-coded sepsis against SOFA-window scoring\DIFdelbegin \DIFdel{on real cohorts}\DIFdelend .
Only detector~2's leakage and detector~5's ablation are injected, because the
quantity \DIFdelbegin \DIFdel{being }\DIFdelend detected is a dose.

\label{sec:taxonomy}

\DIFadd{We define a }\emph{\DIFadd{diagnostic}} \DIFadd{as a procedure that audits an ML pipeline for one
confound and returns a verdict. Our catalogue is initial: it organizes the
failures we found by the stage of a diagnostic at which they arise, and we claim
completeness only with respect to that stage model. A diagnostic has four
stages: (i) a statistic $S$ computed from the audited artifacts; (ii) a decision
rule mapping $S$ to a verdict; (iii) controls that validate the pair; and (iv)
the design process that produced them. The types are:
}

\textbf{\DIFadd{Type 1, vacuous pass}} \DIFadd{(stage ii--iii interface). A check that never
reached a verdict is scored as ``clean''. }\emph{\DIFadd{1a, falsy-as-clean}}\DIFadd{: the verdict
function is total on a domain where $S$ is partial, so an undefined result
(}\texttt{\DIFadd{nan}}\DIFadd{, }\texttt{\DIFadd{None}}\DIFadd{, an empty container) is consumed by a boolean test.
}\emph{\DIFadd{1b, degenerate control}}\DIFadd{: a control with margin $m>3$ (or an identity) is
counted as a true negative though it cannot fail.
}

\textbf{\DIFadd{Type 2, structural non-discriminability}} \DIFadd{(stage ii). For two
implementations that a domain convention deems interchangeable, the population
value $\theta^*$ of the statistic satisfies $|\tau-\theta^*|/\sigma_{\text{audit}}\le 1$.
The flag probability, approximately $\Phi((\tau-\theta^*)/\sigma_{\text{audit}})$, is then
far from 0 and 1 at that audit size. A larger audit would make the verdict
deterministic but still decided by a margin far smaller than the spread of
$\theta^*$ across legitimate implementations; the issue is the threshold's
position relative to that band, not sample size}\DIFaddend .

\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}

We \DIFdelbegin \DIFdel{set out to build confound detectors for clinical machine learning and did:
five of them, precision $1.00$, FPR $0.00$ and recall $0.86$ on the
demonstration-scale internal suite, validated against real historical defects
and re-run at full dataset scale. Auditing those detectors the way
they audit a pipeline produced the more portable result --- }\DIFdelend \DIFaddbegin \DIFadd{built five confound detectors and audited them. The audit gives an initial
catalogue of }\DIFaddend four failure modes\DIFdelbegin \DIFdel{a
diagnostic exhibits independently of whether its target confound is real, each
with a measured instance, one of
them structurally present in a widely used
non-clinical benchmark that guards against it elsewhere in the same file. }%DIFDELCMD < 

%DIFDELCMD < %%%
\DIFdelend \DIFaddbegin \DIFadd{, organized by the stage at which they arise,
with evidence of different strength per type (}\tableref{tab:evidence}\DIFadd{), most of
it unfavourable to our own detectors. }\DIFaddend The practical recommendation is narrow and
cheap: give every diagnostic an explicit undecidable state, check that every
control \DIFdelbegin \DIFdel{is a measurement rather
than }\DIFdelend \DIFaddbegin \DIFadd{has a margin and is not }\DIFaddend an identity, report uncertainty at the operating
point, and fix the decision rule before looking.
\DIFdelbegin \DIFdel{Each caught something in this project.
