# Pre-registration, v2, addendum 2: E7a, a blind language-model rater

Committed before any rater output exists. It extends `PREREG_V2.md` and addendum 1.

**Question.** Does a second rater who has not seen our labels assign the same labels to the 53 model-selection sites and the 88 reachability sites?

**Rater.** A fresh language-model instance with no access to this conversation, to the label files (`task3b_labels.json`, `task4c_labels.json`) or to
any results file. It receives only the blind sheets generated by `v2/scripts/make_rating_sheets.py` (site order shuffled with a fixed seed), the
committed rubric (`RUBRIC_3B.md` or `RUBRIC_4C.md`), and the pinned third-party repositories, which it may read with `git show <sha>:<path>`. One
instance per sheet. It writes one JSON file per sheet. This rater is not independent of the first labeller in the way a human would be, because the
first labels were produced by a language model; the paper says so and reports this agreement separately from any human rater.

**Outputs per site.** Sheet A: label (CONTAMINATED, DISCLOSED_ORACLE, SOUND, NOT_SELECTION, AMBIGUOUS), selection split, reported split, one-line note.
Sheet B: label (GUARDED, UNREACHABLE, REACHABLE_UNGUARDED, NOT_APPLICABLE), diverged-only or ordinary for REACHABLE_UNGUARDED, one-line note.

**Analysis, fixed now (`v2/scripts/e7_agreement.py`).** Per sheet: raw agreement with an exact Clopper-Pearson interval, Cohen's kappa on the label, the
confusion table and every disagreement with both notes. Sensitivity (derived, not a relabelling): the Type 1 counts under the rater's labels on the narrow
and broad readings, and the detector 3 counts of contaminated and real selection sites. Reading rule: if kappa is below 0.60 on either sheet the paper
reports the external counts as rubric-dependent and gives the range across the two raters; if both are 0.60 or above it reports the agreement and says that the
counts are stable to the rater. Disagreements are listed in the appendix and are not adjudicated by us.

**Not done here.** A human rater (E7) is still wanted; its labels, when they exist, are analysed with the same script and reported alongside.
